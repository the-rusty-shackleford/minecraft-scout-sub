# D-0001 — The Scout

nfx's brief (2026-10-07, Submersibles' `knowledge/sources/`): a one-seat submarine, fast, cheap and
fragile, a 9×1 locker, a single narrow spotlight, two upgrade slots and one weapon slot; "a one-man
wet-bike sub, bubble canopy, single prop, one spotlight"; crafted at a crafting table. A Vanilla
Wheels vehicle on Submersibles, life-size, as the Explorer (its D-0001).

**Decision**:

- **Shape.** One block a metre: a pod 3.0 long, 1.4 across and 0.96 deep (a superellipse section,
  exponent 2.4, a rounded nose and a tail tapering to a third), an ellipsoid bubble over the cabin
  rising to 1.8, skids. Yellow from the factory (`#f2c230`), dyeable: a small fast thing should be
  seen. No reference: nfx's words and the role.
- **A voxel shell** (the shared `tools/bbgen/voxel.py`), on single voxels: at 3 metres, two-voxel
  steps were too coarse for a pod 1.4 across, and single ones are 531 boxes. The pod and the bubble
  are one solid, hollowed through the cabin: glass wherever the bubble stands clear of the pod, a
  dark rim where they meet. The glass is named in the profile's `cockpit` as well (Vanilla Wheels'
  D-0031): from the pilot's seat the bubble is not drawn and the view is open all round; before,
  its stepped faces stacked across the whole view in a mosaic (the booth's photo).
- **The seat**: low in the pod, hips 0.5 up, the eye at 1.52 in the bubble; the stick and a small
  panel ahead of it.
- **The spotlight is the nose**: its foremost voxels within 0.14 of the axis are the lamp (range 16)
  in a dark ring, as a torpedo's nose. The voxel grid starts a quarter of a metre ahead of the nose,
  so nothing sits on its edge (an edge counted as touching the glass, and the nose came out rim).
- **The locker**: a chest's single row at 0.4 scale, hidden in the solid tail under a hatch behind
  the bubble; the tail's hit box covers the hatch (a click from above, as from a dock or swimming,
  reaches the chest); the inventory key opens it from the seat.
- **The tail**: one screw in a duct that four swept fins carry, the fins stopping short of the
  blades' disc; a torpedo tube under the chin between the skids, its muzzle the profile's weapon.
- **The draft is 0.45**: at the surface the upper pod and the bubble are out.
- **Numbers**: nfx's proposed stats in Submersibles' terms: top speed 0.44 blocks a tick (thrust
  0.030, slip 0.020, hull drag 0.044), twice the Explorer's; climb 0.14; turn 3.5; spool a second;
  fuel 0.8 a tick of 24 000; durability 1.5; currents push it whole; stabilizer 0.05, so it rolls
  into turns and settles slowly. Tuned in the playtest.
- **Crafting**: the pod at a crafting table from three glass, three copper blocks and a barrel
  (copper, as the brief's own recipe has it: cheap); the pod and an engine there give the packed
  submarine, the pod matched by its vehicle component.
- **The sound**: a submarine engine's steady whirr from a vintage library (CC0), tonal so that its
  climb with the throttle is heard, under the Explorer's deeper hum
  (`devtools/art/sounds/SOURCES.md`).

**Why**:
- A bubble over a low seat is what a one-man submarine is; the open view is the point of it.
- Fragile and fast: a light pod loses its way quickly and reaches top speed in a second.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *An open wet-bike, the rider in the water*: Submersibles' crew breathe aboard (its D-0001); a
  rider drawn in the open would look drowned. The bubble keeps "wet-bike" to the riding position.
- *A torpedo's popping propeller for the sound* (675798): noise with no tone to climb.

**Verification**:
- GameTests (8): the profiles; the recipes, and another vehicle's pod refused; a crafted Scout set
  down on open water floats at its draft; it holds its depth, then floats; its top speed; its pilot
  breathes at depth and a second is refused; the locker through the tail's hatch and from the seat;
  a torpedo from the chin's tube.
- Mutations of the shipped data, each caught: the recipe taking any pod; the tail's hit box
  removed; the draft.
- The booth (16 checks): it floats at its draft, its yellow shows, a real Left Shift dives it and
  Shift held keeps the pilot aboard, the screw turns, it holds its depth, the pilot breathes, the
  pilot sees water through the bubble, the spotlight lights the seabed at night, the use key fires a
  torpedo, R lets the pilot out over the bubble, the fit-out's two and one slots, the locker's row.
